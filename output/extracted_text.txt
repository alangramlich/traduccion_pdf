**E.1) Exchange the tubings**

--- bloque @(77,121) ---
Tubings that are used with the heater-cooler
must be replaced once a year.
1.1

--- bloque @(365,122) ---
Include the tubings in the disinfection as described.
Connect the tubings during the disinfection cycle
to the heater-cooler.

--- bloque @(338,121) ---
1.2

--- bloque @(334,789) ---
NOTE: Only use tubings that are certified
for drinking water systems, such as SORIN
part number 75-510-218.

--- bloque @(72,481) ---
**F.1) Take water samples**

--- bloque @(77,518) ---
Disinfect the drain ports (spray, wipe).
1.1
Only if disinfection has not been performed correctly,
water samples shall be taken and analyzed.
Open the drain port. Let the water run for a while
(5sec) and then take the water sample out of the
running water stream.

1.2
